About Iloilo

History and Culture

Iloilo is a province in Western Visayas in the Philippines. It is known for its rich history, heritage churches, traditional festivals, and beautiful architecture.

Ilonggo Hospitality

The people of Iloilo are known for their warmth, friendliness, and welcoming culture.

Famous Local Food

Iloilo is famous for La Paz Batchoy, Pancit Molo, and other delicious Ilonggo dishes.

Dinagyang Festival

The Dinagyang Festival is a popular celebration in Iloilo City featuring colorful performances, music, and cultural traditions.
