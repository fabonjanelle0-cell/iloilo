TERMS AND POLICY

Explore Iloilo Safely and Responsibly

1. Introduction

Welcome to Explore Iloilo, a tourism information website that introduces visitors to the tourist attractions, historical landmarks, natural beauty, and culture of Iloilo Province, Philippines.

By using this website, visitors agree to follow the terms and policies stated on this page.

2. Purpose of the Website

This website provides general tourism information to help visitors discover destinations and activities in Iloilo. It is intended for educational and informational purposes and is not an official government tourism website.

3. Responsible Tourism Policy

Visitors are encouraged to:

  • Keep tourist spots clean and dispose of waste properly.
  • Respect local communities, traditions, and religious practices.
  • Protect historical landmarks and cultural sites.
  • Avoid damaging plants, wildlife, and natural environments.
  • Follow local rules and safety instructions.
  • Respect the privacy of other visitors and residents.
  • Support local businesses and communities.

4. Tourist Safety Policy

Visitors should check weather conditions, opening hours, transportation, entrance fees, and travel advisories before visiting a tourist destination.

Follow safety signs and avoid restricted or dangerous areas when visiting beaches, islands, mountains, and historical attractions.

5. Accuracy of Information

We aim to provide useful tourism information. However, prices, schedules, opening hours, and destination conditions may change. Visitors should confirm important details with the relevant destination or local tourism office before traveling.

6. Privacy Policy

If visitors contact the website administrator and provide personal information, it should be handled responsibly and only for the intended purpose. External websites may have separate privacy policies.
